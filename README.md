# Pose Coach

Practise yoga in front of your camera and get live feedback on your form, right in your browser.

## What it does

- **Recognises your pose.** Pick a pose, step into view, and Pose Coach tells you how closely you match it.
- **Tells you what to fix.** Short, plain tips like "Bend your left knee more" appear as you move, with the most important one first.
- **Times your hold.** The timer runs while your form is good and resets when it slips.
- **Remembers your best.** Your longest hold for each pose is saved on your device.
- **Speaks to you (optional).** Turn on spoken tips to hear corrections without looking at the screen.

## Poses

Downward dog, goddess, plank, tree and warrior.

## Privacy

Everything runs on your device. Your video is never recorded or uploaded.

## Works on

Phones, tablets and computers, in light or dark mode. It needs a camera and a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

## Tips for best results

- Place your camera about 2 metres away so your whole body is in view.
- Face a window or lamp, not the light behind you.
- Wear fitted clothes so your body shape is easy to see.

*Not medical advice. Stop if anything hurts.*
